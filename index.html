Coin Dash - lab 3

Two players race to collect yellow coins. Dodge red drones and the orange hunter that chases you: if one catches you, you're out, but your rival plays on. Who has more coins after 45 seconds?

Player 1 (teal): arrow keys · Player 2 (purple): WASD · Pause: P · Restart: R · Menu: M
